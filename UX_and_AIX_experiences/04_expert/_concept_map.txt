CONCEPT MAP - 04_expert (README tier: BLUE Expert)
====================================================
The machinery behind the doors advanced used: cold boot, the external
persistence mesh, the mutation-research plane, the codegen room where an
agent writes code into a running world, the admission plane that lets
several of them mutate it at once, the staging half of a hot swap, and
the documents the package hands an agent before any of it boots -
prose it addresses, and structure it walks.
29 lessons authored, 01-29 sequential.
