CONCEPT MAP - 01_beginner (README tier: GREEN Beginner)
=========================================================
The core runtime: bind, resolve, categorize, scope, clean up.
Static conjure only. Existences: unique / many / unique_per_conduit.
Strings preferred over enums. No dynamic mode, no permissions, no
scanning, no lambdas, no spellspaces - those live upstairs.
