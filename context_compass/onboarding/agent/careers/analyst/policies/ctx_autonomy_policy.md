# ctx_autonomy_policy

Purpose
- Apply CTX Autonomy ranking to analyst ctx work and reviews.
- Keep ctx outputs grounded in verifiable sources.

Policy
- Use the CTX Autonomy rubric when reviewing or producing ctx summaries.
- Do not accept ctx below 60; return/defer until the source layer is improved.
- Require traceability to file/dir ctx paths for every claim in component/architecture ctx.

Analyst emphasis
- Fidelity: confirm statements against source ctx, not assumptions.
- Traceability: every summary should point to a supporting ctx path or matrix entry.
- Coherence: resolve contradictions before approving higher-level ctx.
