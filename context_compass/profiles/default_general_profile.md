Purpose
- Provide a baseline profile that is broadly reusable.

General rules
- Unknown-first claims: no promotion to fact without evidence.
- Keep changes scoped and reviewable.
- Prefer deterministic workflows and explicit handoff state.
- Require durable notes during active work.

Recommended policy knobs
- Keep Ticket Microcycle enabled by default.
- Require note usefulness scoring and improve weak notes.
- Require explicit closure confirmation before moving tickets to completed.
