# Context Is Gold (Skill)

## Priority
- Treat durable context as the highest priority for this repo.
- Preserve and enrich it before and after work.

## Re-entry Ritual (Required)
- After context compaction or a fresh session, read:
  - `context_compass/system_docs/src_architecture.md`
  - `context_compass/system_docs/src_components.md`
- Review the diagrams and information sources before making changes.

## Behavior
- Update "Context / Handoff Summary" in active tickets.
- Keep documentation grounded in source evidence.
- Do not handwave; record unknowns and next verification step.
